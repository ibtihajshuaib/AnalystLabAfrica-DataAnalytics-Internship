# DATA ANALYTICS INTERNSHIP – ANALYSTLAB AFRICA 

This repository contains my projects and learning outputs completed during my **Data Analytics Internship with AnalystLab Africa**.

## PROJECTS 

### Week 1 – Telco Customer Churn Analysis

Analysed customer churn patterns and factors associated with customer retention and churn using Excel.

### Week 2 – Superstore Sales & Performance Analysis

Used Power BI to analyse sales, profitability, customer segments, products, and regional performance.

### Week 3 – Superstore Advanced Business Analysis

Performed advanced business analysis using Power BI and DAX to evaluate sales, profit, products, customers, discounts, regions, and time-based performance.

### Week 4 – HealthConnect Appointment Attendance & No-Show Analysis

Conducted an initial analysis and data-quality assessment of appointment attendance and no-show data. Identified relevant variables and business questions and proposed KPIs and an analysis approach.

### Week 5 – HealthConnect Exploratory Data Analysis

Conducted exploratory data analysis on the HealthConnect Appointment Dataset using Power BI. Developed 4 KPIs, created 13 analytical visualisations, and built a two-page dashboard to explore appointment attendance and no-show patterns.

## Tools & Skills

**Tools:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Excel

**Skills:** Data Cleaning, Data Quality Assessment, Data Analysis, Business Analysis, Data Visualization, KPI Development, Business Insights
